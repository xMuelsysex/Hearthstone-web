Card frame assets

Retrieved: 2026-09-06

The PNG frame, slot, material, and font files in this directory are copied from
src/assets at the source repository. Card art files used by the prototype are
from HearthstoneJSON's original-art endpoint:
https://art.hearthstonejson.com/v1/orig/RLK_843.png
https://art.hearthstonejson.com/v1/orig/CS2_082.png

These assets are used by the card-frame renderer in the prototype page and live
game card faces. The frame source is a Hearthstone-derived fan resource; it is
not an official Blizzard asset. The two prototype art files are lightly prepared
from the same original-art sources to remove endpoint padding/background pixels
before fitting the frame art windows.
The materials/masks PNGs are cropped from the MIT material assets and are used
as numeric-area covers where the official card image's baked values need to be
hidden. Complete attack, vitality, and weapon durability material PNGs are used
for stat slots whose full silhouette is part of the live card layer.
`weapon-durability-cover.png` and `weapon-attack-cover.png` are small raster
occlusions generated from the clean weapon stat-plate texture; they fill the
corresponding material's hollow center so baked numerals cannot show through
while the live number remains on top.
